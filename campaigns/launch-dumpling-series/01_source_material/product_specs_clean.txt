The Model 150 is the upgraded high-capacity solution for large-scale commercial use. With enhanced roller-pressing tech and up to 10,000pcs/h output, it delivers unmatched efficiency and consistent production quality, outperforming standard 130-type machines in durability and speed.
Key Advantages
✅ Versatile Production:
Easily switch between Dumplings, Wontons, Spring Rolls, Samosas, Empanadas, and Potstickers by simply changing the molds.
✅ Precision Engineering:
Upgraded frequency control system allows precise adjustment of wrapper thickness (0.5-3.0mm) and filling amount.
✅ Authentic Texture:
Uses upgraded roller pressing instead of screw-extrusion, preserving gluten elasticity for a chewy, handmade taste.
✅ High-Moisture Filling Support:
The "sheet-first, fill-later" design handles chunky meat and vegetable fillings that screw-type machines cannot.
